About FoodShare

Connecting Food Donors with Verified NGOs to Reduce Hunger and Food Waste.

Our Mission

Every day, tons of perfectly good food are wasted while millions of people struggle with hunger. FoodShare is a platform that bridges this gap by connecting food donors with verified NGOs that distribute food to people in need.

Together, we can reduce food waste and make sure surplus food reaches the right hands.

Why Choose FoodShare?
